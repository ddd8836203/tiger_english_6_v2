## Part C: Write Right 3 (Unit 6) — Guided Biography Writing from Outline

> **大題說明**：
> `C. George wrote the following outline. Help him to write his story in full down below.`
> `Write complete sentences. You may need to add certain words to the outline, and think about your verb tenses:`
>
> 📋 **【提綱 Outline (Layer 1 Ground Truth)】**：
> - `Person I admire: Leonardo da Vinci`
> - `Occupation: artist`
> - `Country: Italy`
> - `Birth: 1452`　`Death: 1519`
> - `Accomplishments:`
>   - `- make many great drawings & paintings`
>   - `- paint the “Mona Lisa”`
> - `Thanks to him: can enjoy some really great art`
> - `Why admire? - gifted and talented; drew almost 2,500 drawings,`
>   - `- one of the greatest artists of his time`
>
> **大題得分**：`18 / 20`
