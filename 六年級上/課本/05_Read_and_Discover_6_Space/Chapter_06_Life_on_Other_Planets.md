# Read and Discover 6: Space — Chapter 06: Life on Other Planets

> [!INFO] **【全書脈絡導航 (Book Context & Syllabus Anchor)】**
> - **所屬教材**：Oxford Read and Discover Level 6: All About Space（全書共 53 頁）
> - **教材定位**：國小六年級上學期（CLIL 跨領域天文科普與天體生物學長篇精讀，CEFR B1）
> - **全書總綱**：[00_Overview.md](file:///C:/@植善課業/AI/英語學習網頁/六年級上/課本/05_Read_and_Discover_6_Space/00_Overview.md)
> - **章節進度**：第 6 章 / 全書共 8 章（地外生命探尋、火星運河迷思、冰封衛星與系外行星）
> - **學術思維遞進線**：
>   - **前置章節**：Chapter 5 天文學歷史、哥白尼與牛頓、哈伯太空望遠鏡與銀河系。
>   - **本章焦點**：天體生物學 (Astrobiology) 與地外生命三要素（液態水 liquid water、碳 carbon、大氣氣體 oxygen & nitrogen）；百年前火星運河 (canals) 與火星人 (Martians) 科幻迷思；火星地下水與探測車 (rovers/robots)；木衛二歐羅巴 (Europa) 冰下海洋；土衛六泰坦 (Titan) 碳氫化合物 (hydrocarbons) 甲烷湖泊與大氣層；系外行星 (exoplanets) 與幽浮 (UFOs) 真相。
>   - **後續銜接**：Chapter 7 太空中的人類 (People in Space / International Space Station & Moon Landings)。
> - **出題防護約束**：嚴格辨析四大星體環境與生命條件：
>   - `Earth`：富含液態水、大氣層（氧氣與氮氣）、多樣化生命與細菌。
>   - `Mars`：極度低溫，表面水分皆為冰，科學家推測土壤或地下暗河有液態水；現有地球機器人探測中。
>   - `Europa (木衛二)`：木星衛星，表面覆蓋厚冰層，內部因潮汐加熱較溫暖，冰層下方可能有液態海洋與微生物。
>   - `Titan (土衛六)`：土星最大衛星，太陽系「唯一擁有濃密大氣層的衛星」；地表有碳氫化合物組成的河流、湖泊與降雨；地下亦可能有水。

## 第一層：課本 100% 原文高保真 (Ground Truth)

### 1. Introduction: Life on Other Planets (Student Book p.24)

In stories, aliens are living things from another world, but are they real? Is there life on other planets? Astronomers are looking for life in our solar system, and on planets around other stars.

> **[Photo Caption p.24]**: `From the Movie Mars Attacks!` *(圖解：科幻電影《星戰毀滅者》中成群外星幽浮飛碟蜂擁飛向地球的太空場景)*

---

### 2. Martians! (Student Book p.24)

A hundred years ago, telescopes were smaller, and it was difficult to see Mars clearly. Astronomers thought that they saw lines on the surface of Mars, and some people thought that they were canals. At the time, people were building great canals on Earth, like the Panama Canal in Central America.

Some astronomers imagined aliens building these canals and living in cities on Mars. People wrote stories and made movies about aliens from Mars, called Martians, visiting Earth. Now we know that there aren't any Martians, but we still make movies about them!

---

### 3. Water and Bacteria (Student Book p.25)

Life on Earth needs liquid water, carbon, and gases like oxygen and nitrogen. When scientists look for life on other planets, they look for places that have liquid water.

Bacteria are simple living things on Earth, and they can live in very hot and cold places, and even inside rocks. Scientists are looking for bacteria on Mars and in other places in our solar system.

> **[Microscope Photo Caption p.25]**: `Bacteria` *(圖解：顯微鏡下分裂繁殖的綠色桿狀單細胞微生物細菌)*

---

### 4. Life on Mars (Student Book p.25)

It's very cold on Mars, so all the water on the surface is ice. Many scientists think that there is liquid water in the soil or even in underground rivers. Maybe there is life on Mars, too. At the moment, robots from Earth are exploring Mars and looking for life.

> **[Photo Caption p.25]**: `A Robot on Mars` *(圖解：火星地表紅色沙漠荒原中行駛探測的無人科學探測車機械手臂與全地形輪胎)*

---

### 5. Europa (Student Book p.26)

Europa is one of Jupiter's moons and it's covered in ice. The surface is very cold, but scientists think it's warmer inside. Maybe there are oceans under the ice. Maybe there are bacteria or other living things in the oceans, too. One day people will send robots to explore Europa and to look for life.

> **[Photo Caption p.26]**: `Europa` *(圖解：木衛二歐羅巴巨大球體，表面佈滿縱橫交錯的冰裂紋理，在深邃太空中反射微弱陽光)*

---

### 6. Titan (Student Book p.26)

Saturn's largest moon, Titan, is the only moon in the solar system with atmosphere. It has rivers, lakes, rain, and clouds. These are made of chemicals called hydrocarbons, and scientists think that there is water under the surface of Titan, too. Living things are made of hydrocarbons and water. Maybe one day we will discover life on Titan.

> **[Photo Caption p.26]**: `Titan` *(圖解：土衛六泰坦星球，邊緣環繞著濃密黃橙色混濁霧霾大氣層)*

---

### 7. Life in Other Solar Systems & Discover! Box (Student Book p.27)

What do we know about life in other solar systems? Astronomers have found hundreds of planets near other stars, but they are still looking for a planet like Earth. Some scientists think that there are lots of planets like Earth in our galaxy, and millions in the universe. Maybe there is life on some of them. On another planet billions of kilometers from here, maybe alien astronomers are looking at the stars!

> [!NOTE] **Discover! Box (p.27)**
> Sometimes, people see unusual objects in the sky, called UFOs. Some people think that they are alien spacecraft, but they are usually things like balloons or comets. *(圖解：模糊晃動的碟狀發光物體在夜空中劃過)*

### 8. Chapter 6 Activities (Student Book p.46 - p.47)

#### Activity 1: Write the words. (p.46)
*Word Bank*: `UFO` | `canal` | `bacteria` | `alien`

1. *(圖片：兩側有石岸步道與小船的人工開鑿運河)* ➔ **canal**
2. *(圖片：綠色大頭大眼睛的漫畫外星人形象)* ➔ **alien**
3. *(圖片：高倍顯微鏡下群聚分裂的微生物單細胞群)* ➔ **bacteria**
4. *(圖片：發光懸浮於夜空的金屬圓盤飛碟)* ➔ **UFO**

#### Activity 2: Write Earth, Mars, Europa, or Titan. (p.46)
1. These places have an atmosphere. ➔ **Earth**, **Mars**, and **Titan** *(解析：地球擁有富氧大氣；火星有稀薄二氧化碳大氣；泰坦為太陽系唯一擁有濃密大氣層的衛星；歐羅巴無顯著大氣)*
2. The surface of these places is ice. ➔ **Mars** and **Europa** *(解析：火星表面溫度極低，表面水分皆為冰；木衛二歐羅巴表面全面封凍著厚重冰層)*
3. Robots are exploring this place. ➔ **Mars** *(解析：課本明載 At the moment, robots from Earth are exploring Mars)*
4. These places have lakes on the surface. ➔ **Earth** and **Titan** *(解析：地球表面有水湖；泰坦表面有液態碳氫化合物甲烷乙烷湖泊)*
5. Lots of living things are in this place. ➔ **Earth** *(解析：地球是目前唯一已知充滿繁盛生命的大本營)*

#### Activity 3: Complete the sentences. (p.47)
*Word Bank*: `imagine` | `covers` | `underground` | `simple`

1. Bacteria are **simple** living things on Earth.
2. We can **imagine** life on other planets.
3. Maybe there are **underground** rivers on Mars.
4. Ice **covers** the surface of Europa.

#### Activity 4: Circle the correct words. (p.47)
1. Life on Earth needs **solid** / **liquid** water. ➔ **liquid**
2. At the moment, robots are exploring **Venus** / **Mars**. ➔ **Mars**
3. Scientists think that the inside of Europa is **warmer** / **colder** than the surface. ➔ **warmer**
4. The lakes on Titan are made of **oxygen** / **hydrocarbons**. ➔ **hydrocarbons**

#### Activity 5: Answer the questions. (p.47)
1. **Why did people think there were canals on Mars?**
   - **Model Answer**: `Because telescopes were smaller a hundred years ago, so astronomers thought they saw lines on the surface of Mars, and people were building great canals on Earth.`
2. **Why isn't there any water on the surface of Mars?**
   - **Model Answer**: `Because it's very cold on Mars, so all the water on the surface is ice.`
3. **What are living things made of?**
   - **Model Answer**: `Living things are made of hydrocarbons and water (and they need carbon, liquid water, and gases like oxygen and nitrogen).`
4. **Do you think we will find life on other planets? Why or why not?**
   - **Model Answer (Free Open Thinking)**: `Yes, I think we will find life because there are billions of planets in the universe and scientists believe some of them have liquid water and organic chemicals.`
