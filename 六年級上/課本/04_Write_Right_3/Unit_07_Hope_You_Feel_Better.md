# Write Right 3 — Unit 07: Hope You Feel Better

> [!INFO] **【全書脈絡導航 (Book Context & Syllabus Anchor)】**
> - **所屬教材**：Write Right Level 3
> - **教材定位**：國小六年級上學期（慰問卡書寫格式、傷病事故過去式表達、關懷與主動幫忙句型、節日卡片介系詞）
> - **單元進度**：第 7 單元 / 全書共 8 單元
> - **寫作技能遞進線**：
>   - **前置能力**：Unit 6 歷史偉人傳記寫作與過去式敘事。
>   - **本課焦點**：慰問卡 (Get-Well Card)——四大結構：
>     1. **Greeting（問候稱謂）**：`Hi [Name]! How are you feeling?`
>     2. **Body（內文兩段）**：
>        - Paragraph 1（詢問缺席原因＋轉述聽聞傷病與就醫狀況＋表達遺憾與希望早日好轉）：`I was wondering why you...` / `I heard (from ...) that you...` / `I also heard that you...` / `I hope...`
>        - Paragraph 2（推測對方當下感受＋主動提供協助＋表達同學想念與盼望歸隊）：`You must be...` / `Do you need anything?` / `If you want, I can...` / `Everyone in our class misses you.` / `We all want you to...`
>     3. **Closing（祝福收尾語）**：`Get well soon!`
>     4. **Signature（寄卡人署名）**：`Danny`
>   - **後續銜接**：Unit 8 食譜步驟與祈使句寫作 (How to Make Spaghetti)。
> - **出題防護約束**：
>   - **傷病過去式轉換**：提綱給定原形動詞片語，寫入慰問卡描述「已經發生的受傷或就醫經歷」時，必須全數轉為過去式（尤其不規則動詞 `broke`、`caught`、`cut`、`hurt`、`fell`、`ate`、`had`、`got`、`were` 與雙寫 `slipped`）。
>   - **不定冠詞精準度**：母音開頭名詞片語搭配 `an`（`an eye infection`、`an x-ray taken`、`an allergic reaction`）；子音開頭搭配 `a`（`a cast`、`a rash`、`a black eye`、`a car accident`）。
>   - **五大句型完整度**：不可遺漏 `want you to` 的不定詞 `to`；`Everyone` 當主詞搭配單數動詞 `misses`；`must be + 形容詞`（人感到無聊用 `bored`，切勿誤用 `boring`）。
>   - **節日介系詞鐵律**：`at Christmas`（無 `Day` 節慶期間用 `at`）vs. `on Valentine's Day` / `on Mother's Day` / `on New Year's Day`（含 `Day` 之特定節日一律用 `on`）。

## 第一層：課本 100% 原文高保真 (Ground Truth)

### 1. Warm-up

- **Writing Task**: Write a get-well card.
- **8 Core Injury & Illness Phrases (八大常見傷病片語)**:
  1. `break your leg`（摔斷你的腿）
  2. `sprain your ankle`（扭傷你的腳踝）
  3. `burn your arm`（燙傷／燒傷你的手臂）
  4. `catch the flu`（得流感）
  5. `cut your finger`（割傷你的手指）
  6. `bruise your face`（撞傷瘀青你的臉）
  7. `hurt your knee`（弄傷你的膝蓋）
  8. `have an eye infection`（眼睛發炎／感染）

- **Discussion Questions**:
  1. *How often do you get sick?*（你多久生病一次？）
  2. *When were you last sick? What were your symptoms?*（你上次生病是什麼時候？當時有什麼症狀？）

### 2. Model Text: Danny's Card

#### Danny's Card (Original Text)
```text
Hi Penny! How are you feeling?
I was wondering why you missed school this past week. Then I heard from Jean that you fell down the stairs and broke your leg. I also heard that you were hospitalized for a few days. How awful! I hope your leg doesn't hurt too much.
You must be bored staying at home all day. Do you need anything? If you want, I can bring you some comic books. Everyone in our class misses you. We all want you to come back.
Get well soon!
Danny
```

#### Authentic Chinese Translation (繁體中文精準對照)
嗨，佩妮！你現在感覺怎麼樣？
我一直在想你過去這一週為什麼沒來上學。後來我從珍那裡聽說你從樓梯摔下來而且摔斷了腿。我也聽說你住院住了幾天。真糟糕！我希望你的腿不會太痛。
你整天待在家一定很無聊。你需要什麼嗎？如果你想要的話，我可以帶一些漫畫書給你。我們班上每個人都很想你。我們都希望你趕快回來。
祝你早日康復！
丹尼 敬上

#### Answer the Questions (課後問題與標準解析):
1. **How did Penny break her leg?**
   - *Standard Answer*: `She fell down the stairs (and broke her leg).`
   - *Parsing*: 使用過去式不規則動詞 `fell`（原形 `fall`）說明事故原因。
2. **For how long was Penny hospitalized?**
   - *Standard Answer*: `She was hospitalized for a few days.`
   - *Parsing*: 過去被動式 `was hospitalized` 搭配持續時間片語 `for a few days`。

### 3. Writing Basics I

**Look at the pictures. Complete the sentences using the phrases in the box. (Remember to change the verbs into the *past tense*.)**

- **Phrase Box**: `hurt your knee` / `break your leg` / `catch the flu` / `sprain your ankle` / `bruise your face` / `have an eye infection` / `burn your arm` / `cut your finger`
- **Completed Sentences**:
  1. `You broke your leg.`（`break` $\rightarrow$ `broke`，不規則過去式）
  2. `You hurt your knee.`（`hurt` $\rightarrow$ `hurt`，三態同形）
  3. `You bruised your face.`（`bruise` $\rightarrow$ `bruised`，規則加 `-d`）
  4. `You caught the flu.`（`catch` $\rightarrow$ `caught`，不規則過去式）
  5. `You had an eye infection.`（`have` $\rightarrow$ `had`，不規則過去式；母音開頭用 `an`）
  6. `You sprained your ankle.`（`sprain` $\rightarrow$ `sprained`，規則加 `-ed`）
  7. `You burned your arm.`（`burn` $\rightarrow$ `burned`，規則加 `-ed`）
  8. `You cut your finger.`（`cut` $\rightarrow$ `cut`，三態同形）

### 4. Writing Basics II

**Look at the pictures. Write the sentences using the phrases in the box. (Remember to change the verbs into the *past tense*.)**

#### Part A: Accidents & Causes (`I heard that you...`)
- **Phrase Box**: `slip on the ice` / `eat rotten food` / `fall down the stairs` / `be in a car accident` / `spill hot water`
- **Completed Sentences**:
  1. `I heard that you fell down the stairs.`（`fall` $\rightarrow$ `fell`）
  2. `I heard that you slipped on the ice.`（`slip` $\rightarrow$ `slipped`，短母音單子音雙寫 `p` 加 `-ed`）
  3. `I heard that you spilled hot water.`（`spill` $\rightarrow$ `spilled`）
  4. `I heard that you were in a car accident.`（`be` $\rightarrow$ `were`，配合主詞 `you`）
  5. `I heard that you ate rotten food.`（`eat` $\rightarrow$ `ate`，不規則過去式）

#### Part B: Medical Treatments (`I also heard that you...`)
- **Phrase Box**: `have an x-ray taken` / `get a cast on your leg` / `be hospitalized` / `have to wear an eye patch` / `have surgery`
- **Completed Sentences**:
  6. `I also heard that you were hospitalized.`（`be` $\rightarrow$ `were`）
  7. `I also heard that you had an x-ray taken.`（`have` $\rightarrow$ `had`；`an x-ray`）
  8. `I also heard that you had surgery.`（`have` $\rightarrow$ `had`；`surgery` 作不可數名詞不加冠詞）
  9. `I also heard that you got a cast on your leg.`（`get` $\rightarrow$ `got`）
  10. `I also heard that you had to wear an eye patch.`（`have to` $\rightarrow$ `had to`）

### 5. Writing Practice I

#### Part A: `I was wondering why you + [Past Tense]...`
**Look at the example. Write the sentences in the same way. (Remember to change the verbs into the *past tense*.)**
- **[Example]** `(miss school)` $\Rightarrow$ `I was wondering why you missed school.`
- **Practice Sentences**:
  1. `(go to the nurse's office)` $\Rightarrow$ `I was wondering why you went to the nurse's office.`
  2. `(leave school early)` $\Rightarrow$ `I was wondering why you left school early.`
  3. `(be crying in pain)` $\Rightarrow$ `I was wondering why you were crying in pain.`
  4. `(be taken to the hospital)` $\Rightarrow$ `I was wondering why you were taken to the hospital.`
  5. `(faint suddenly)` $\Rightarrow$ `I was wondering why you fainted suddenly.`

#### Part B: `You must be + [Adjective]...`
**Complete the sentences using the words in the box.**
- **Word Box**: `sad` / `bored` (Example) / `sleepy` / `hurting` / `embarrassed` / `excited`

- **Practice Sentences**:
  1. `You must be excited` `to travel abroad.`
  2. `You must be sad` `to transfer to another school.`
  3. `You must be embarrassed` `to have a stain on your shirt.`
  4. `You must be sleepy` `after staying up all night.`
  5. `You must be hurting` `after hitting your head.`

### 7. Organizing & More Expressions

#### Graphic Organizer (Penny's Card Model)
- **Greeting**: `To whom?` $\rightarrow$ `Penny`
- **Body**:
  - **What happened to Penny**:

### 8. Drafting & Writing Tip!

#### Drafting Template
```text
Greeting:
Hi [ Penny ], [ How are you feeling ]?

Body:
(Paragraph 1)
I was wondering [ why you missed school this past week ]. Then, I [ heard from Jean ] that you [ fell down the stairs and broke your leg ]. I also heard [ that you were hospitalized for a few days ].
[ How awful ]! I hope [ your leg doesn't hurt too much ].

(Paragraph 2)
You must be [ bored staying at home all day ].
Do you [ need anything ]? If you want [, I can bring you some comic books ].
[ Everyone in our class misses you ]. We all want [ you to come back ].

Closing:
[ Get well soon! ]

Signature:
[ Danny ]
```

#### Writing Tip! Writing Cards (卡片書寫場合與節日介系詞)
> [!IMPORTANT] **Writing Tip! (Writing Cards)**
> You can write a card for many occasions. With a nice card, you can congratulate someone's **birthday, anniversary, graduation, wedding** or **new job**. You can also send a card **at Christmas**, **on Valentine's Day**, **on Mother's Day** or **on New Year's Day**. When someone is **sick** or **going away**, you can send your best wishes through your card.

- **介系詞黃金對比**：
  - **`at Christmas`**：`Christmas` 本身無 `Day`，表示聖誕節慶期間用介系詞 `at`（若寫 `Christmas Day` 則改用 `on Christmas Day`）。
  - **`on Valentine's Day` / `on Mother's Day` / `on New Year's Day`**：凡節日名稱包含具體某「天」（`Day`）者，一律搭配介系詞 `on`。
