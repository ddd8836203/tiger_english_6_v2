# Write Right 3 — Unit 06: A Great Person in History

> [!INFO] **【全書脈絡導航 (Book Context & Syllabus Anchor)】**
> - **所屬教材**：Write Right Level 3（全冊共 77 頁）
> - **教材定位**：國小六年級上學期（歷史傳記寫作、過去事實敘述與因果總結邏輯）
> - **單元進度**：第 6 單元 / 全書共 8 單元
> - **寫作技能遞進線**：
>   - **前置能力**：Unit 5 條件句與金額分配記敘 (If I Had a Lot of Money)。
>   - **本課焦點**：歷史偉人傳記文章 (Biography Essay)——三段式結構：
>     1. **Introduction**：引導出最欽佩的對象 (`The person I admire the most is...`)
>     2. **Body**：個人生平與成就 (身分/國籍/出生年份/重大成就/最高代表作/深遠影響：`Thanks to..., we can...`) + 欽佩原因 (`However, I admire... most of all because...`)
>     3. **Conclusion**：因果總結詞 (`For these reasons, I think... was a great person.`)
>   - **後續銜接**：Unit 7 社會議題與說服性短文。
> - **出題防護約束**：
>   - 出生與過世句型：`was born in [year] and died in [year]`（主詞單數用 was，年份介系詞用 in）。
>   - 功勞歸屬句型：`Thanks to [Person], S + V...`（注意介系詞用 to，切勿誤用 for 或 of）。
>   - 最高代表作句型：`His/Her most famous [invention/composition/painting/accomplishment] is [Noun/V-ing]...`（主詞為單數名詞，be 動詞用 is）。
>   - 轉折與欽佩原因句型：`However, I admire [Person] most of all because he/she [past verb]...`。

### 1. Warm-up (Student Book p.46)

- **Writing Task**: Write about a great person in history.
- **7 Great Historical Roles & Their Key Actions**:
  1. **inventor** — `invent many things`（發明家——發明許多事物）
  2. **composer** — `compose great musical pieces`（作曲家——創作偉大的樂曲）
  3. **scientist** — `make scientific discoveries`（科學家——做出科學發現）
  4. **political leader** — `lead the nation`（政治領袖——領導國家）
  5. **writer** — `write great stories`（作家——創作偉大的故事）
  6. **philosopher** — `teach the meaning of life`（哲學家——傳授生命的意義）
  7. **artist** — `make great works of art`（藝術家——創作偉大的藝術作品）

- **Discussion Questions**:
  1. *Who do you admire?*（你欽佩誰？）
  2. *What did this person do?*（這個人做了什麼事？）

### 2. Model Text: Ella's Story (Student Book p.47)

#### The Person I Admire
```text
The person I admire the most is Thomas Edison.
Thomas Edison was an inventor from the United States. He was born in 1847 and died in 1931. Edison invented many things. His most famous invention is the light bulb. Thanks to Edison, we can see at night.
Edison was smart and wise. However, I admire Edison most of all because he never gave up on his dreams. He failed many times before he invented the light bulb.
For these reasons, I think Thomas Edison was a great person.
```

#### Authentic Chinese Translation (繁體中文精準對照)
我最欽佩的人是湯瑪斯·愛迪生（Thomas Edison）。
湯瑪斯·愛迪生是一位來自美國的發明家。他出生於 1847 年，並於 1931 年逝世。愛迪生發明了許多事物。他最著名的發明是電燈泡。多虧了愛迪生，我們在夜晚才能看得見。
愛迪生既聰明又充滿智慧。然而，我最欽佩愛迪生的地方在於他從不放棄自己的夢想。在發明電燈泡之前，他經歷了無數次的失敗。
基於這些原因，我認為湯瑪斯·愛迪生是一位偉大的人。

#### Answer the Questions (課本課後問題與標準解析):
1. **Who was Thomas Edison?**
   - *Standard Answer*: `Thomas Edison was an inventor from the United States.`
   - *Parsing*: 採用過去式 was 與不定冠詞 an (因 inventor 為母音開頭)，標註國籍 from the United States。
2. **What did Edison do?**
   - *Standard Answer*: `He invented many things. His most famous invention is the light bulb.`
   - *Parsing*: 過去式動詞 invented，並具體點出最著名代表作 the light bulb。

### 6. Writing Practice II (Student Book p.51)

#### Part A: Look at the example. Write the sentences in the same way.
- **Example**: `(Edison / never give up on his dreams)` $\Rightarrow$ `I admire Edison most of all because he never gave up on his dreams.`
- **Practice Sentences**:
  1. `(Mozart / study music very hard)` $\Rightarrow$ `I admire Mozart most of all because he studied music very hard.`
  2. `(Keller / never complain about her disabilities)` $\Rightarrow$ `I admire Keller most of all because she never complained about her disabilities.`
  3. `(Gandhi / be always very patient)` $\Rightarrow$ `I admire Gandhi most of all because he was always very patient.`
  4. `(da Vinci / be curious about many things)` $\Rightarrow$ `I admire da Vinci most of all because he was curious about many things.`
  5. `(Newton / be not afraid to be different)` $\Rightarrow$ `I admire Newton most of all because he was not afraid to be different.`

#### Part B: Unscramble the sentences.
1. `admire / The person / is / the most / I / Thomas Edison / .`
   $\Rightarrow$ `The person I admire the most is Thomas Edison.`
2. `things / Edison / many / invented / .`
   $\Rightarrow$ `Edison invented many things.`
3. `before / He failed / the light bulb / many times / he invented / .`
   $\Rightarrow$ `He failed many times before he invented the light bulb.`
4. `think / a / Thomas Edison / I / great / is / person / .`
   $\Rightarrow$ `I think Thomas Edison is a great person.`

#### Drafting Template:

```text
Body:
(Paragraph 1)
[ Thomas Edison ] was [ an inventor ] from [ the United States ].
He was born [ in 1847 ] and died [ in 1931 ].
He [ invented many things ].
His most famous [ invention ] is [ the light bulb ].

(Paragraph 2)

Conclusion:
For these reasons, [ I think Thomas Edison was a great person ].
```

#### Writing Tip! Transition Words: Consequence
> [!IMPORTANT] **Writing Tip! (因果轉折詞：Consequence)**
> Transition words and phrases can be used to **show the effect or result** of something. They often show how different ideas logically connect.
> Here are some examples:
> - `for these reasons`（基於這些原因）
> - `therefore`（因此）
> - `as a result (of this)`（結果；因此）
> - `because (of this)`（因為這個緣故）
> - `as a consequence`（作為其結果）
> - `consequently`（結果；必然地）
> - `so`（所以）
> - `thus`（因而；由此）
