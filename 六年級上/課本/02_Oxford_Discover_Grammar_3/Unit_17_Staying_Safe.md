#### A. Listen and read. 🎧 Track 33

# What Do You Do in a Hurricane?

**Before a Hurricane**
- Bring garden furniture into the house.
- Put boards over your windows.
- Get some money from the bank.
- Get a first-aid kit, some cans of food, and some bottles of water from the supermarket.

**During a Hurricane**
- Go into a building and stay there.
- If your home is in a low area, go up to a higher place or go to a hurricane shelter.
- Listen to the radio. If it's time to get out of the area, follow instructions and leave.

**After a Hurricane**
- Don't drive through flooding water.
- Don't walk under damaged trees, buildings, or bridges.

#### C. Correct the mistakes. Write the correct instructions.
*(依據颶風防災常識與課文內容，修正錯誤指示並寫出正確祈使句：)*

1. Bring garden furniture out of the house.
   ➔ **Bring garden furniture into the house.**
   *(解析：颶風來前若將花園家具搬出屋外會被強風吹飛砸傷人，應搬「進」屋內 into the house。)*

2. Put boards under your windows.
   ➔ **Put boards over your windows.**
   *(解析：木板應釘蓋「在窗戶上方表面」over your windows 以阻擋強風與飛散物，而非放在窗戶下方 under。)*

3. Listen to CDs during a hurricane.
   ➔ **Listen to the radio during a hurricane.**
   *(解析：颶風期間可能斷網或需要接收政府撤離廣播，應收聽廣播電台 the radio，而非聽音樂光碟 CDs。)*

4. Go to a lower place if your home is in a low area.
   ➔ **Go up to a higher place (or go to a hurricane shelter) if your home is in a low area.**
   *(解析：低窪地區極易淹水，應往「高處」higher place 或颶風避難所移動，不可往更低處 lower place。)*

### 【p.86 學習文法 Learn Grammar — Imperatives】

#### A. Read and learn.
*(核心文法規則框：Imperatives 祈使句)*
*(插圖說明：右上角有一隻橘色圓滾滾的小火苗精靈微笑站立。)*

> **To form the imperative, use the base form of a verb. Use the imperative to tell people to do something.**
> - **Go** into a building.
> - **Stay** there!
>
> **Use *don't* + imperative to tell people not to do something.**
> - **Don't leave** your home.
> - **Don't drive** through flooding water.
>
> **When you use imperatives to give instructions, you often add *please*.**
> ***Please* can go at the beginning or the end of a sentence:**
> - **Please** stay there.
> - **Please** don't move.
> - Stay there**, please**.
> - Don't move**, please**.

**核心句型結構整理：**
1. **肯定祈使句**：`原形動詞 (Base Verb) + ...`（省略主詞 `You`，直接由原形動詞開頭）
2. **否定祈使句**：`Don't + 原形動詞 (Base Verb) + ...`（不可使用 `Not + V` 或 `Doesn't + V`）
3. **禮貌語氣 `please` 規則**：
   - 放句首：`Please` 直接接原形動詞或 `don't`（不加逗號，如 `Please stay there.` / `Please don't move.`）。
   - 放句尾：`please` 前方**必須加上逗號 `,`**（如 `Stay there, please.` / `Don't move, please.`）。

### 【p.87 學習文法 Learn Grammar — Prepositions of Movement】

#### C. Read and learn.
*(核心文法規則框：Prepositions of Movement 動態方向介系詞)*

> **Use prepositions of movement to show which direction a person or thing is moving in.**

| 編號 | 動態介系詞 | 紙箱／階梯圖示軌跡說明 | 中文核心語意 |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **into** | 紅色箭頭由紙箱外部上方弧線進入箱內 | 進入⋯⋯裡面（由外向內） |
| **2** | **out of** | 紅色箭頭由紙箱內部向外側弧線飛出 | 從⋯⋯出來（由內向外） |
| **3** | **up** | 紅色箭頭沿著階梯斜線往上指 | 向上；往高處 |
| **4** | **down** | 紅色箭頭沿著階梯斜線往下指 | 向下；往低處 |
| **5** | **over** | 紅色箭頭從紙箱左側上方跨過弧線到達右側 | 越過；跨越上方；覆蓋於上 |
| **6** | **under** | 紅色箭頭從紙箱左側底部繞行穿越下方到達右側 | 從⋯⋯下方穿過；在下方 |
| **7** | **from** | 紅色箭頭從紙箱側邊往外延伸離開 | 從⋯⋯出發／來源起點 |
| **8** | **to** | 紅色箭頭從遠處指向並接近紙箱 | 朝向⋯⋯；到達目的地 |
| **9** | **around** | 紅色箭頭沿著紙箱外圍左右兩側環繞 | 繞過；沿著外圍行進 |
| **10** | **through** | 紅色箭頭直線穿透中間簍空的立體紙箱通道 | 穿過；貫穿內部空間 |

#### E. Where can you go in an emergency? Find the places on the map. Complete the directions from the police station to the school.
> 📷 **【地圖格局描述】**：
> - **左下角（底層橫向道路旁）**：**POLICE**（警察局），緊鄰後方通往公園入口的白色階梯（steps）。
> - **左側中央大綠地**：有鐵欄杆圍繞與拱門標示 `PARK` 的公園，公園內有一條彎曲灰色小徑通往一條弧形深色大馬路。
> - **頂部橫向道路**：**DRUGSTORE**（藥局）位於公園東北側的頂部道路旁。
> - **中央偏右跨路／河道地標**：一座灰色拱形**橋樑（bridge）**連接弧形馬路與右側建築街區。
> - **右側街區（由上至下沿縱向道路排列）**：最上方為 **FIREHOUSE**（消防局）、正對橋樑中央為 **SCHOOL**（學校）、最下方為 **HOSPITAL**（醫院）。

**對話填空與正解：**
- **A**: Excuse me. How do you get ¹ **from** the police station ² **to** the school?
- **B**: Go ³ **up** the steps. Walk ⁴ **through** the park. Go along the road. Go ⁵ **over** the bridge.
- **A**: Thanks!

*(解析：`from A to B` 表示從起點 A 到終點 B；從警察局後方爬階梯進公園用 `up`；沿小徑穿過公園內部用 `through`；從橋面上跨越到達學校用 `over`。)*
