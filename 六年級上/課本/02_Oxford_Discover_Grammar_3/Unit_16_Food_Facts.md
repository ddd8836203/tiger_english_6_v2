### 【p.81 學習文法 Learn Grammar】

#### A. Read and learn.
*(核心文法規則框：Adjectives with Prepositions)*
*(插圖說明：右上角有一隻翠綠色圓滾滾的植物小精靈正微笑揮手。)*

> **Some adjectives form phrases with prepositions.**

| Adjective (形容詞) | Preposition (介系詞搭配與受詞形式) | 中文核心語意 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **happy / excited / confused / worried / sorry / concerned / sure / curious / angry / sad** | **about** (something / doing something) | 對於某事/做某事感到高興、興奮、困惑、擔憂、抱歉、關切、確定、好奇、生氣、難過 |
| **happy / bored / disappointed** | **with** (something / someone) | 對某人/某事物感到滿意、厭煩、失望 |
| **nice / kind / good / bad / stupid / silly / smart / sensible / rude** | **of** (someone (to do something)) | 某人（做某事）真是太好/善良/愚蠢/聰明/明智/無禮了 |
| **interested / disappointed** | **in** (something / someone) | 對某事物/某人感興趣、感到失望 |
| **surprised / amazed / confused** | **by** (something or someone) | 對某事物或某人感到驚訝、驚奇、困惑 |
| **good / bad / excellent** | **at** (doing something) | 擅長 / 不擅長 / 專精於（做某事） |
| **full** | **of** (something) | 充滿某物（滿滿都是...） |
| **famous** | **for** (something) | 以某特產/特徵而聞名 |

4. for / **of** ➔ **[of]** *(full of fat)*
5. **about** / with ➔ **[about]** *(angry about the number of chemicals)*
6. on / **of** ➔ **[of]** *(bad of them to use)*
7. in / **about** ➔ **[about]** *(happy about that)*
8. **about** / of ➔ **[about]** *(excited about the food)*

### 【p.82 篇章練習與除錯 Practice】

#### E. Complete the text with the words in the box.
> 📷 **【插圖描述】**：
> 一間溫馨寬敞的家庭廚房。大姊（穿綠色長袖上衣、金黃色長髮）手拿打蛋器正在金屬大碗裡認真攪拌蛋糕糊；身旁八歲的小妹妹 Lisa（留著金色雙肩短髮、穿藍色洋裝）正端著一個白色馬克杯遞給姊姊。料理台上有牛奶壺、打散的雞蛋殼與雞蛋盒，前方放著兩包拆封的調味料紙袋，袋上分別寫著大大的「SUGAR」與「SALT」。餐桌上擺著一張木椅與一盆紫紅色鮮花。

> **選詞框：**
> `of` | `at` | `of` | `about` | `by` | `about` | `in` | `about`

**文章填空與正解標註：**

Hi! My name's Lisa and I'm eight. Yesterday, my sister and I made a cake. I'm not very good **¹ [at]** cooking, but my sister is interested **² [in]** cooking and she makes great things! I was excited **³ [about]** making the cake. I wanted my family to be surprised **⁴ [by]** how delicious it was. I gave my sister the ingredients and she mixed them together in a big bowl. We put the cake in the oven. I was worried **⁵ [about]** burning the cake, so I watched it all the time. When the cake was ready, my sister tried a little piece. "Ugh!" she said. "It's full **⁶ [of]** salt!" I couldn't believe it. I had given my sister a cup of salt, not a cup of sugar. That was very silly **⁷ [of]** me. I was very sorry **⁸ [about]** making that mistake, but my sister thought it was funny!

**答案解析：**
1. **at** ➔ be good at + V-ing（擅長於料理）
2. **in** ➔ be interested in + V-ing（對烹飪感興趣）
3. **about** ➔ be excited about + V-ing（對做蛋糕感到興奮）
4. **by** ➔ be surprised by + 名詞子句（對蛋糕有多美味感到驚喜）
5. **about** ➔ be worried about + V-ing（擔心蛋糕烤焦）
6. **of** ➔ be full of + 名詞（充滿鹽巴）
7. **of** ➔ silly of me（我真是太糊塗愚蠢了）
8. **about** ➔ be sorry about + V-ing（為犯了那個錯誤感到抱歉）

#### F. Correct the mistakes. Write the correct sentences.
*(找出句中介系詞搭配錯誤並重寫完整正確句子)*

1. It was nice with Grandma to make us a cake.
   ➔ **It was nice of Grandma to make us a cake.**
   *(解析：評價某人的人格特質或善意行為，用 It is nice of sb to do sth，不可用 with。)*

2. I'm disappointed of these cookies. They're not very nice.
   ➔ **I'm disappointed with these cookies. They're not very nice.** *(或 disappointed in)*
   *(解析：對具體物品或表現感到失望，固定搭配 disappointed with 或 in，不可用 of。)*

3. My brother isn't interested about cooking.
   ➔ **My brother isn't interested in cooking.**
   *(解析：interested 固定搭配介系詞 in，不可誤用 about。)*

4. We're surprised of how much sugar there is in fruit juice.
   ➔ **We're surprised by how much sugar there is in fruit juice.**
   *(解析：受到某事實的震驚或驚訝，搭配 surprised by 或 at，不可用 of。)*

5. Italy is famous about its pizza and pasta.
   ➔ **Italy is famous for its pizza and pasta.**
   *(解析：以某特色/產物聞名，固定搭配 be famous for，不可用 about。)*

6. The children are excited with going to a restaurant for dinner.
   ➔ **The children are excited about going to a restaurant for dinner.**
   *(解析：對即將發生的事情感到興奮期待，固定搭配 excited about，不可用 with。)*
