# My Journey with Captain Magellan

> 📖 **【故事導言 (Story Introduction)】**  
> *Juan is a ten-year-old boy traveling on Ferdinand Magellan's ship, the Trinidad. Magellan is an explorer from Portugal who wants to sail west to find a new route to Indonesia. Europeans go to Indonesia to trade for spices. Juan writes about his trip in his journal.*

#### 【課文正文：啟航、大西洋風暴與麥哲倫海峽 (1519–1520)】

**September 20, 1519**  
We sail from Spain today, with 250 men and five ships! The sky is blue, and the **clouds** are white. I am not afraid. Captain Magellan is a brave man. I brought him his dinner of salted beef and beans. He gave me a coin.

> 💭 **Think: Where is Juan on September 20, 1519? Point to the map.**  
> *(思考題答案：Juan is in Spain, getting ready to sail across the Atlantic Ocean.)*

The five ships **sail** toward South America. The **journey** is long. There are many **storms** with thunder and **lightning**. In November, the ships can't move because there is no wind to fill the sails.

**November 10, 1519**  
The ocean is different today. It looks like glass. Every day, Captain Magellan says, "Juan, go up to the deck and check the sails." I always go up and **look around** and bring back the same news. There is no wind.

The ships reach the **coast** of South America in December 1519. They stay there for one year. Then they sail through a narrow area of water called a **strait** and reach the Pacific Ocean. They lose one ship in a storm. Another ship sails back to Spain.

**November 27, 1520**  
We are in a new ocean! Captain Magellan calls it the Pacific Ocean. I am sad to leave this land. Winter was cold and **windy**, with **fog** and **hail**, but I saw many **rivers** and beautiful **waterfalls**. Now it is spring. It is time to sail west again.

> 💭 **Think: Where is Juan on November 27, 1520? Point to the map.**  
> *(思考題答案：Juan has sailed through the Strait of Magellan at the southern tip of South America and just entered the Pacific Ocean.)*

- **地圖標示**：`ATLANTIC OCEAN`, `Spain`, `South America`, `Rio de Janeiro`, `Strait of Magellan`, `PACIFIC OCEAN`（搭配八方位羅盤 `N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW`）。

We are home today, after three long years. I am now 13 years old. I will never forget Captain Magellan. I really **looked up to** him. I have sailed around the world. I have seen many new places and learned so much. I'm excited to tell everyone about my travels.

#### 【Word Study: Phrasal Verbs with Look (`look` 動詞片語)】

```text
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 📌 Learn: Phrasal Verbs with Look (look 動詞片語學習框)                     │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ We can use look with prepositions to make phrasal verbs.                    │
│ (我們可以把動詞 look 搭配介系詞或副詞，組成意思不同的片語動詞。)            │
│                                                                             │
│ • I go up and look around. (我走上甲板環顧四周。)                           │
│ • Look around means that Juan looks in all directions.                      │
│   (look around 的意思是 Juan 朝著四面八方張望。)                            │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

> 🎧 **Listen and read the phrasal verbs. Use each one in a sentence. (Track 3.09)**

1. **look around** — 環顧四周；四處張望 *(look in all directions)*
2. **look through** — 瀏覽；翻閱；仔細查看 *(read or examine quickly from start to end)*
3. **look for** — 尋找 *(try to find something missing)*
4. **look up** — 抬頭看；查閱（字典／資料） *(raise one's eyes / search for information in a book or online)*
5. **look after** — 照顧；照料 *(take care of someone or something)*
6. **look out** — 小心！注意！／向外看 *(be careful of danger / watch out)*
*(課文延伸：`look up to` 敬仰、崇拜 — "I really looked up to him.")*

#### 【Writing Study: Complex Sentences Using Before and After】

```text
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 📌 Learn: Complex Sentences Using Before and After (before/after 複合句)    │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Remember: Before is what happens first. After is what happens second.       │
│ In a sentence, where you put the events changes if you use before or after. │
│ (記住：Before 連接先發生的動作概念方向；After 引導先發生的事件之後才做某事。)│
│                                                                             │
│ 原始順序：1st I studied hard.   2nd I took the test.                        │
│ • [1st] I studied hard  before  [2nd] I took the test.                      │
│ • [2nd] I took the test  after  [1st] I studied hard.                       │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### 【隨堂圈選練習完整原文】

- **Read the sentences. Circle *before* or *after*.**
  1. I brushed my teeth `[ before / after ]` I went to bed. ➔ **before** *(先刷牙 1st，再去睡覺 2nd)*
  2. I ate an apple in our kitchen `[ before / after ]` I got home. ➔ **after** *(先回到家 1st，才在廚房吃蘋果 2nd)*
